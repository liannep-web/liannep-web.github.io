About

Hello, I'm Lianne

I design and build responsive websites

Having worked as a graphic artist, web designer and web developer for 16 years. Front-end development is my greatest passion and I enjoy the challenge of building websites especially ones with complex designs and dynamic content.

I am a skilled with extensive experience in crafting responsive, accessible, and high-fidelity digital assets, including websites, landing pages and email marketing collaterals. Proven ability to translate complex designs into robust front-end solutions, leveraging HTML, CSS, and JavaScript within enterprise environments such as Salesforce Marketing Cloud, WordPress, and Shopify

Adept at streamlining development workflows through strategic application of scripting, dynamic content, and reusable components, while consistently ensuring brand consistency and optimal user experience. Experienced in deploying tracking and measurement codes, maintaining design systems, and collaborating cross-functionality to achieve project goals.

I believe that a strong foundation in web design and development principles, including responsive web design, and adapting to new technologies and platforms is very important in the work that I do.
